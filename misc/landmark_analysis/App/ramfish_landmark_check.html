RAMFISH Landmark Check

Measures how far landmarks sit from their Round 1 positions before alignment and after rough and fine alignment. Everything stays on this computer.

Load the Round 1 image plus, for each later round, the image before alignment (1_normalized), after rough alignment (ALIGNED_…) and after fine alignment (R#_aligned_…). Any channel of a round works; pick one where the landmarks are clear.

Session

A session or landmark file keeps the landmarks, names and pixel sizes. Images are not stored: re-add them and they attach by file name. Results are calculated from the points alone, so a landmark file can be re-analyzed without the images.

Landmark file columns: specimen, round, stage, landmark, X, Y (optional landmark_name, image, pixel_size_um). Stage: ref (Round 1), unaligned, rough or fine.

By round (current specimen)

Figure: images, landmarks, distances and close-ups

Uses the current specimen: the reference image and the images of one aligned round (the round of the image you have open, otherwise the first round). Keep those images loaded. Reference positions are green, aligned positions magenta.

Statistics

Paired two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Grouped by landmark: one value per specimen and round. Grouped by specimen, and for "All": the mean of each round's landmarks (landmarks of the same round are not independent). With 6 rounds the smallest possible p is 0.031.

Distances are measured from each landmark's Round 1 position, in µm. "Points" has the same columns as the landmark file.
